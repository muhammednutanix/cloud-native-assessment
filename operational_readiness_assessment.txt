FAQ
What is the Cloud Native Readiness Assessment?
The Cloud Native Readiness Assessment is a one-minute benchmark tool that helps organizations evaluate their container operational readiness, identify operational gaps across VMs and Kubernetes, and receive a tailored roadmap for scaling enterprise data services.

How can platform engineering scale cloud native operations?
Platform engineering scales cloud native operations by standardizing lifecycle management, automating Day-2 operations like logging and storage, and providing developers with unified self-service capabilities.

Who should take this assessment?
This assessment is designed for IT leaders, platform engineers, and cloud architects who are managing or planning to manage unified operations across virtual machines, Kubernetes, and enterprise data services.
